PERSONAL HISTORY

Je-Gun Joung is an Associate Professor of Biomedical Science and Head of Systems Life Sciences at CHA University, and leads the Omics Team at the Longevity Research Center, Bundang CHA Hospital. He previously worked as a Principal Researcher at the Samsung Genome Institute and received his Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Seoul National University.
