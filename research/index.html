Research

Our lab develops computational approaches to understand how genomic and molecular heterogeneity shapes disease and cellular function. By integrating multi-omics data (genome, transcriptome, single-cell, and spatial profiles) with machine learning, we aim to discover clinically meaningful biomarkers and to support the development of precision oncology and cell therapies.

Research areas

  • Cancer genomics & tumor evolution — intratumor heterogeneity, clonal evolution, and molecular subtypes in gynecologic, colorectal, lung, and liver cancers
  • Tumor immune microenvironment — single-cell and spatial profiling of immune landscapes and their association with treatment response
  • Multi-omics of stem cells — large-scale integrative analysis of ESCs and MSCs for cell therapy quality and function
  • AI/ML-driven marker discovery — interpretable models linking genetic variation and molecular profiles to clinical and cellular phenotypes
